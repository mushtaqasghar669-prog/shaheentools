Privacy Policy

Shaheen Tools is designed so that many tools can process text and local files directly in your browser. The site may save preferences such as favorites, workout setup, workout streak and recent local history in browser storage.

Camera assistance is optional. When enabled, the browser camera stream is displayed locally. This implementation does not intentionally upload the camera video to Shaheen Tools servers. Camera permissions remain controlled by your browser/device.

Browser text-to-speech uses the speech facilities provided by the device/browser. Availability and language quality can vary by device.

If Firebase Analytics is enabled in your deployment, analytics data may be processed according to Firebase/Google policies.

Google advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies. Google requires publishers to disclose relevant third-party vendors, advertising cookies and available personalization opt-out information.

Users can manage Google advertising preferences through Google's advertising settings. Where applicable, a Google-certified consent management platform should be configured for EEA, UK and Switzerland traffic before personalized ads are served.

Terms of Use

Tools are provided for general informational and productivity purposes. Results should be checked before being used for important financial, legal, medical, engineering or safety decisions.

Do not use Shaheen Tools to violate law, bypass authorization, abuse systems, deceive people or infringe intellectual property.

Health & children

Shaheen Tools avoids body-size targets, calorie restriction and appearance-based fitness goals in the youth-oriented workout experience. The focus is comfortable movement, mobility, coordination, general fitness and consistency.

Younger users should use age-appropriate activities and involve a parent, guardian, teacher or other trusted adult when appropriate.
