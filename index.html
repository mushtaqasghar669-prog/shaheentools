About Shaheen Tools

Shaheen Tools is a browser-first utility platform. It combines practical calculators, text utilities, developer helpers, creator utilities, learning tools and a safe home-movement experience.

Privacy

Many tools in this version process input locally in your browser. Gym setup, streak and workout history are stored in localStorage on this device. Camera video is used locally for the optional camera experience and this code does not upload or save the camera stream.

If Firebase Analytics, Google services, advertising or other third-party services are enabled on the deployed site, their respective privacy practices also apply. Review and configure your Privacy & Messaging/CMP settings before serving personalized advertising where required.

Terms

Tools are provided for general informational and productivity purposes. Outputs should be checked before being used for important decisions. Shaheen does not guarantee that every calculation or generated result is suitable for professional, legal, financial or medical decisions.

Kids & Teens

Shaheen's youth-oriented movement features avoid weight-loss targets, appearance comparisons and extreme challenges. They focus on movement, coordination, mobility, basic strength and healthy routines.

Suggested product modes are: Little Kids (1–4), Kids (5–8), Preteen (9–12), and Teen Basics (13–15). These labels are product modes rather than medical classifications.

AdSense transparency

Advertising should remain clearly distinguishable from site controls. Active workout screens, camera controls and interactive tool runners are intentionally kept free of manually inserted ad units.

Google AdSense approval is never guaranteed by application code. Final approval depends on the live site's content, policies, privacy setup, navigation, traffic and Google's review.
