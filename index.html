Privacy

Workout preferences, streaks and history are stored locally in your browser unless you later add a separate cloud feature. Camera processing in this version is performed in the browser; the page does not intentionally upload or record the camera feed.

Google advertising and analytics technologies may process data according to their respective policies and your consent choices.

Advertising

Ads are kept separate from navigation and interactive controls. Never ask users to click an ad and never design an ad to look like a tool button, download button or navigation element.

Fitness safety

This is general fitness software, not medical care. Use a comfortable pace. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or feel unwell, and seek appropriate guidance from a parent/guardian or qualified health professional when needed.
